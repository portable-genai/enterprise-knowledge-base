Cloud Provider Onboarding Policy

This policy applies to every engagement with an external cloud provider used to process
or store bank data, in any region.

Scope and ownership

The Retail Technology function owns this policy. Deviations are recorded in the register.
Due diligence before onboarding

Before a cloud provider is onboarded the bank completes a security review, a resilience
assessment and a data residency assessment, and records the outcome in the vendor file.

Provider tier   Review depth   Refresh cycle
Tier one        full           annual
Tier two        standard       biennial
