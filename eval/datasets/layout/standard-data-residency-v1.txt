Data Residency Standard

This standard governs where customer records may be stored and processed. It carries a
restricted classification.

Applicability

The standard applies to all restricted customer data held by the bank.

Storage location for restricted customer records

Restricted customer records must be stored and processed only in the in-country region,
and a copy must never be replicated to another jurisdiction without an approved waiver.

Region              Permitted        Waiver required
in-country          yes              no
other jurisdiction  no               yes
