AML Customer Onboarding Policy

This policy sets the anti-money-laundering checks a relationship manager completes for
every new customer of the bank.

Purpose

The policy exists to keep customer onboarding within the financial crime risk appetite.

Required AML checks for customer onboarding

At onboarding the bank screens the customer against sanctions and politically exposed
person lists, verifies identity documents, and establishes the source of funds before the
first transaction is released.

Check                 Owner                Timing
Sanctions screening   Financial Crime      at onboarding
Source of funds       Relationship Manager before first transaction
