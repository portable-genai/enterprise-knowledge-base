Incident Response Runbook

This runbook covers the handling of a major technology incident affecting customer
service or bank data.

Severity definitions

A major incident is one that degrades a customer-facing service or exposes bank data.

Notification of the risk function during a major incident

The risk function is notified within thirty minutes of a major incident being declared,
and a written update follows every hour until the incident is closed.

Controls applying to the incident response process

Every declared incident is logged, assigned an owner, and reviewed after closure.
